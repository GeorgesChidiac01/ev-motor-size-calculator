Motor and drivetrain

Electrical and battery

Model. Road load F = ½ρCdA(v+vwind)² + mg sinθ + Crrmg cosθ. Tire radius r = (25.4D + 2W·AR/100)/2000 m. Motor speed n = 60Gv/(2πr), torque T = Fr/(Gηgear). Acceleration power from Ehsani et al., Modern Electric, Hybrid Electric and Fuel Cell Vehicles: P = δm(vf²+vb²)/(2t) + ⅔mgCrrvf + ⅕ρCdAvf³. Motor envelope is constant torque up to base speed, then constant power to max speed (base = max / CPSR). Rotor volume = peak torque / TRV; stator OD = rotor diameter / split ratio; motor mass ≈ 2 × active mass at 6000 kg/m³. Battery energy = range × consumption at the range speed (flat, no wind) / usable fraction / (1 − margin). Voltages assume SVPWM in the linear region (peak phase = Vdc/√3).

Reference point: a Nissan Leaf (2012) motor is roughly 80 kW, 280 N·m, 10,390 rpm max, with about a 198 mm stator OD and 151 mm stack.
